Core Laboratory Capabilities

Mechanical Testing

Destructive testing arrays validating structural boundaries. Standard configurations cover **Tensile verification**, **Bend evaluation parameters**, certified **Impact testing**, and **Hardness matrices**.

Alloy Failure Investigations

Definitive root-cause analysis of structural components, weldments, and heavy processing machinery. High-resolution fractography and microstructure tracking profiles.

Physical Chemistry & Geotechnical

Accurate Material Specific Gravity (SG) profiles and high-resolution Particle Size Distribution (PSD) analytics optimized for process engineering plant control variables.

Moisture Analysis

Quantitative thermal evaluation tracking moisture mass configurations within process stream samples, bulk commodities, and geological material profiles.
