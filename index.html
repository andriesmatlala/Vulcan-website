Independent Metallurgical & Materials Testing Laboratory

Delivering precision mechanical verification, failure investigations, elemental analysis, and process chemistry testing engineered strictly for heavy fabrication, mining operations, and structural sectors.

Core Laboratory Capabilities

Mechanical Testing

Comprehensive destructive testing arrays validating material structural boundaries and regulatory compliance tolerances. Standard configurations cover full **Tensile verification**, **Bend evaluation parameters**, certified **Impact configuration protocols**, and structural **Hardness matrices**.

Alloy Failure Investigations

Definitive root-cause analysis of critical engineering components, structural weldments, and heavy processing plant equipment failures. High-resolution evaluation mapping macro-fractography mechanics and full microstructure tracing profiles.

Physical Chemistry & Geotechnical

Accurate evaluations tracking precise Material Specific Gravity (SG) profiles alongside high-resolution Particle Size Distribution (PSD) analytics. Engineered data processing to guide mining plant operations and milling circuits.

Moisture Analysis

Quantitative thermal evaluation matrix monitoring moisture mass configurations within process stream samples, bulk commodities, and geological material profiles to ensure precise transport compliance and chemical process stability metrics.

Analytical Instrumentation Menu

X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF)

Quantitative high-speed elemental screening arrays and detailed multi-matrix composition mapping protocols for mine processing tailings monitoring, mineral concentrates, and waste stream profiling calculations.

LECO CS Elemental Analysis

Ultra-low precision interstitial Matrix Carbon and Sulfur quantification thresholds. Explicit engineering validation data tracking for strict alloy classification, casting consistency validation, and structural steel grade specifications.
